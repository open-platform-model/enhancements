# Graduation Criteria — Module and Catalog Publishing

This document records the gates that must hold before the enhancement advances along the design lifecycle. Treat these as design acceptance criteria, not implementation milestones — implementation progress lives in `config.yaml.implementation` and the `history` list.

## accepted → implemented

The design is shipped when:

- `opm module publish` and `opm catalog publish` exist as one implementation with two entry points, with test coverage on every refusal path, not only the happy path.
- `opm catalog version set` writes `identity/identity.cue` idempotently, preserving formatting, comments, and any type assertion on the field — with real catalog repos as fixtures rather than synthetic ones.
- `opm catalog registry check` verifies a published catalog against the registry, and the same verification runs when a catalog is added to a `#Platform` registry. Its `--compat` mode (D7) exists and its **help text** says it is an aid rather than a gate — the distinction 0010 D35 rests on belongs where a catalog author meets it, not only in a design document.
- **D9's compatibility gate ships, and it is the field-wise walk rather than `Subsume`.** [`experiments/03-d27-compat-gate`](experiments/03-d27-compat-gate/) measured a single `cue.Value.Subsume` call at 10/14 forward and 8/14 reverse, failing on disjoint sets, and a three-rule field-wise walk at 14/14; a shipped gate built on `Subsume` has reintroduced the measured defect regardless of what it scores on the happy path. Three checks distinguish it: a removed field is refused, a **changed default** is refused (the violation the consumer side provably cannot catch, which is the gate's entire reason to exist), and a bumped `apiVersion` finds no predecessor and passes trivially.
- **The gate is level-aware (0010 D34), and the alpha case is tested.** An `v1alpha1` contract that removes a field publishes successfully; the same removal at `v1beta1` is refused. The alpha test is the one worth writing, because a gate that checks everything passes every beta test while silently contradicting D34.
- **The comparator lives in `library`, not in `cli`.** Publish, `opm catalog registry check --compat`, and any CI action share one implementation — the argument 0010 D32 makes for putting the single-provider guard in the kernel. Predecessor selection **moves** rather than being rewritten: `materialize/enumerate.go`'s `enumerateVersions` plus `filter.go:112`'s `highestStable` already do it, and 0010 D14 deletes `filter.go` out from under them.
- The identity-completeness gate is exercised against a tree with an unfilled field and refuses, with a message naming the field and its file.
- The local-override gate is exercised in all four states: module clean, module blocked, module allowed by flag, catalog blocked with the flag passed and ignored.
- Every catalog repo's copy-and-stamp publish task is **deleted**, not disabled, and its release workflow calls the new command.
- `modules/Taskfile.yml`'s checksum-driven publish and `modules/versions.yml` are deleted, and whatever OQ4 chose is in place and has published at least one real module.
- The owner-scoped namespace migration has run, and the artifacts sharing the namespace without being modules have been dealt with explicitly rather than left behind. The measured set is four kinds, not two: the `library/testdata` fixture, the legacy `v1alpha1` paths, `opmodel.dev/modules/opm-platform` (a `#Platform` published into module space, dev-registry only), and `opmodel.dev/releases/*` (four repositories of Instance-vocabulary artifacts under a segment D5 never reserved). Whatever OQ9 decides is what "dealt with" means for the last two.
- **The `-e2e.g<sha>` prerelease pile in module space is carried or dropped deliberately.** `opmodel.dev/modules/test/podinfo` carries roughly seventy of them (measured 2026-07-31) — enhancement 0006 OQ18's dev-tag pollution in a namespace that entry did not examine. Harmless under 0010 D14, since a platform names one build, but the namespace move either brings them along or leaves them behind, and that should be a choice rather than a side effect.
- A publish has been rehearsed end to end against a non-production registry, including the failure cases, before the first real one.
- `config.yaml.implementation.status = complete` with `date`; `history` names the landing milestones; `README.md` carries the implementation-status quote block and a filled `## Deviations from Design`.
