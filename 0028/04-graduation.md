- **Both experiments have concluded and their outcomes are linked.** [`experiments/01-operator-module-render/`](experiments/01-operator-module-render/) from D2 and D12, and [`experiments/02-cli-bootstrap-install/`](experiments/02-cli-bootstrap-install/) from D3, D4, D8, D9 and D11, each as measured evidence under the decision it constrains. D8 now makes install do all three itself. The same run renders the five unbound ClusterRoles through the catalog's role with no subjects (D12:R2), which experiment 01 rendered as raw objects, and shows them equal to the manifest's apart from labels, and a run interrupted after the Deployment delete is completed by re-running install (D8:R12).
