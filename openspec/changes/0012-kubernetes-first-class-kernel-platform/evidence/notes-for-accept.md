# Notes for the owner at accept

These notes come from the delivered seeds and the conversion. The old entry did not hold them as open questions, so they are not in the proposal.

- **A seed and `deletion-protocol` overlap on a failed delete.** The seed `instance-deploy`, requirement "A failed resource delete keeps the record", says that for a CLI-owned instance a failed delete of one tracked resource leaves the others processed, keeps the record, says re-running is safe and exits 1. The requirement "A frontend deletes only what the plan names" of `deletion-protocol` says a frontend releases an instance's hold only on a release verdict, and "The hold verdict follows policy and outcome" says the verdict follows the policy and the plan's outcome (0012:D1:R5). Both speak of what a failed delete does to the hold or the record. The entry has no scenario for a failed delete now: the "A partial failure" scenario came from `02-design.md` and was removed during the seed review. The owner decides at accept which text owns the rule and whether 0012 keeps its side.
- **`0012:D3:R8` is marked `repo`; it may be a requirement.** The line is a library-internal rule: no loop and no cluster action in the library. It is not a package name, unlike the other eight `repo` lines. The map marks it `repo` and sends it to `cut-requirements.md`. The owner decides at accept whether it stays `repo` or becomes a requirement of 0012.
- **0012 ADDs a protection that the seed `instance-ownership` already states for the CLI prune.** The requirement "Namespaces and CRDs are never deleted automatically" of `deletion-protocol` (scenario "A stale CRD") says both frontends skip a stale Namespace or CRD and name the reason. The seed `instance-ownership` states the same for the CLI prune (scenario "A protected kind"): a stale Namespace or CRD is protected, is not deleted and is listed as left behind. The seed also narrows the rule: a resource of kind Namespace outside the core group is not protected. Once 0012 is delivered, two capabilities hold one rule, a later change to one leaves the other stale, and the history shows two origins. The owner chooses at accept between a MODIFIED of the seed requirement and a narrowing of the 0012 requirement to what the seed does not say.
