# Notes for the owner at accept

This point is a clash that the old text of 0020 holds in its own requirement lines. The conversion is faithful, so it does not rewrite the requirements. The old entry did not hold it as an open question, so it is not in the proposal. The owner decides the wording at accept.

- **A removal that is never refused, and five refusals of a build that carries tombstones.** "A removal itself is never refused" (old 0020:D6:R3) says a build that carries a tombstone for every omitted beta or stable contract publishes, and that the gate refuses the missing record and never the removal. Five other requirements refuse or reject such a build when one of its tombstones breaks their rule: "A withdrawal waits for the seasoning floor" (old 0020:D10:R1, a replacement younger than the floor), "An alpha contract is never tombstoned" (old 0020:D6:R5, an alpha key), "A tombstone states a reason" (old 0020:D9:R1, no reason or an empty one), "A tombstone cannot name itself as its replacement" (old 0020:D9:R3) and "A tombstone repeats its own key" (old 0020:D8:R2, a key field that differs from its map key). A build with such a tombstone satisfies the first requirement and fails one of the others. The scenario "A deliberate removal" is narrowed to a stable key and a tombstone that names no replacement; it still assumes a tombstone that the last three requirements accept. The requirement text of the first is unchanged.
