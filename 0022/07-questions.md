# Open Questions: Machine-Readable Artifact Metadata in cue.mod/module.cue

## Open Questions

Track unresolved questions surfaced during design. Each entry carries a `Status:` line; close it with `resolved-by-D##`, `deferred-to-NNNN`, `deferred-to-implementation`, or `answered` when the question resolves. Each unresolved entry also carries `Blocking:` (`acceptance` with the reason inline, `deferrable`, or `implementation`).

- **OQ1: The `catalogs` rule for catalog dependencies the path cannot classify.** Status: open. Blocking: acceptance (it decides the gate's implied side in `schemas/target.cue`). Today the gate derives `catalogs` from every `deps` key under `opmodel.dev/catalogs/`. A third-party catalog at `example.com/platform-bits@v1` is a `deps` entry the prefix does not classify. Options: the author lists it in `catalogs` and the gate only checks that every listed entry is a dep at its pin (asymmetric: OPM catalogs are implied, others declared); or the gate reads the dependency's own published block and requires `kind: "catalog"` (a registry round-trip per dep at publish); or third-party catalogs are out of scope for the map until one exists.
- **OQ2: Whether `kind` is asserted against the path prefix inside OPM-owned domains.** Status: open. Blocking: acceptance (it is one more implied field in the gate). `opmodel.dev/modules/*` and `templates/*` imply `module`, `catalogs/*` implies `catalog` (0011 D25 makes templates module-kind at publish but a template is what `kind: "template"` names). Asserting agreement catches a copy-pasted block; not asserting keeps the gate path-agnostic. To settle: assert, and whether `kind: "template"` is required or merely allowed under `opmodel.dev/templates/`.
- **OQ3: The annotation key set.** Status: open. Blocking: deferrable. `contracts/contracts.cue` proposes `dev.opmodel.publisher`, `dev.opmodel.cue` and `dev.opmodel.block` (the block key the artifact carries, so a lister can tell blockless artifacts apart without fetching layer 1). To settle: names, whether CUE's `org.cuelang.vcs-*` are always written (requires a VCS at publish) and what a non-VCS publish writes.
- **OQ4: Wording of the 0011 decision that amends D3 and D8.** Status: open. Blocking: acceptance (the amendment is appended to 0011 the moment this entry is accepted, and 0011's bodies are protected, so the text must be agreed here first). Working text: "The version writer keeps every schema-fixed copy of `Version` in step: `identity/identity.cue` and the `custom.\"opmodel.dev@v0\".identity.Version` field of `cue.mod/module.cue`. `--version` fills or asserts both. **Amends:** D3, D8."
- **OQ5: Tidy-then-gate stability.** Status: open. Blocking: implementation. `cue mod tidy` re-encodes the block (sorted keys, normalised scalars). The gate compares values, not bytes, so it should pass after tidy; experiment 01 measures it, and the change that lands the gate claims this question.
